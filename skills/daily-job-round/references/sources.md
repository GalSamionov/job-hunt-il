# Israeli job sources (all fields)

## General boards
- AllJobs (alljobs.co.il): huge volume, all fields. Some postings are VIP/customers-only; skip those. Has bot protection: never automate at speed.
- Drushim (drushim.co.il): all fields. If the user enables auto-send features there, confirm they want it.
- JobMaster (jobmaster.co.il): all fields, strong in admin, sales, logistics.
- LinkedIn Jobs: professional and corporate roles; Easy Apply needs a CV uploaded to LinkedIn.
- Indeed Israel / Glassdoor: aggregators, check freshness.
- JobNet, Jobinfo, Taasuka: smaller boards, varied fields.

## Public sector and large employers
- נציבות שירות המדינה (gov.il, מכרזים): government tenders, strict deadlines and requirements.
- Municipalities and local authorities: מכרזי כוח אדם on each city's site.
- Hospitals, universities, banks, insurers, retail chains, food and industry groups: career pages on their own sites.

## Communities
- Facebook groups by field and region ("דרושים <עיר>", "משרות ל<תחום>").
- WhatsApp/Telegram job groups the user is in (the user shares posts; never join groups on their behalf).
- LinkedIn posts by recruiters ("we're hiring", "מגייסים").

## Placement agencies (חברות השמה)
- Many corporate and mid-level roles go through agencies. A posting may hide the client's name. Flag agency postings and note that the same job can appear several times.

## Field-specific
- Tech: company career pages, Comeet, Greenhouse, Lever boards.
- Education: משרד החינוך, רשתות חינוך.
- Health and care: health funds (קופות חולים), hospitals.
- Hospitality and retail: chains' career pages, seasonal hiring.
