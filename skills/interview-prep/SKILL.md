# Interview prep

> If `job-search/profile.md` does not exist yet, run the job-hunt-onboarding skill first.

Prepare the user for a specific interview in one focused pass, then rehearse if they want.

## Steps
1. Collect: company, role, posting text, interview stage and format (phone, video, in person, panel, home task), interviewer names if known, date and time. Read the profile and the CV version sent.
2. Research the company: what it does, size, news from the last months, culture signals. Cite sources.
3. Produce a prep sheet (short, scannable, in Hebrew):
   - The role in one paragraph, and the 3 things they will check.
   - The user's 60-second introduction tailored to this role.
   - 6 to 8 likely questions with answer outlines built from the user's real experience (STAR: situation, task, action, result).
   - Hard questions for this user (gaps, step-down, short tenure, reason for leaving) with honest, calm answers. Never badmouth a former employer.
   - Salary answer for this role (see recruiter-call references).
   - 4 to 5 smart questions to ask them.
   - Logistics: address or link, what to bring, when to leave.
4. Offer a mock interview: ask one question at a time, wait for the answer, give short feedback, then the next question.
5. After the interview: draft a thank-you message (follow-ups skill) and update the tracker.

## Language and style (always)
- Talk to the user in Hebrew unless they write in another language.
- Never use em dashes. Use commas, colons or separate sentences.
- Do not start a Hebrew line or bullet with an English word; it breaks right-to-left display. Rephrase so the line starts in Hebrew.
- Avoid Latin variables or math notation inside Hebrew lines.
- Be direct and short. No preamble, no restating the request.
